# 07 — Permits, Licenses & Legal/Regulatory

*Not legal advice. Have a Florida real estate attorney and CPA confirm before launch. Several items below were flagged by research as unverified — marked ⚠️.*

## Company formation
- **Florida LLC** via Sunbiz (Division of Corporations): $125; annual report $138.75 by May 1 ($400 late fee).
- Common structure: **Holding LLC** → separate LLCs for (a) flip/operations company, (b) rental holdings (one LLC per property or small group) for liability isolation. Note lenders and insurers may require title in borrowing entity.
- **EIN** (IRS), business bank accounts per entity.
- **Florida Corporate Income Tax** applies to C-corps; LLCs taxed as partnerships/S-corps pass through (no state personal income tax).
- **Beneficial Ownership (FinCEN) reporting** — check current status; FinCEN's **residential real estate reporting rule** (all-cash purchases by entities) may require title companies to report LLC buyers.

## Local business tax receipts (BTRs)
- **Palm Beach County BTR** (Tax Collector, pbctax.gov) — required for business operations; separate **short-term rental BTR** per property, renewed annually by **Sept 30**.
- **City BTR** (Boca Raton / Delray Beach) for the operating business. Rental BTR rules vary city to city in PBC (Riviera Beach, Boynton, WPB require rental BTRs) ⚠️ confirm Boca Raton and Delray specifics with each city's business tax office.
- **Delray Beach** stopped requiring landlord permits/renewals effective **Oct 1, 2023** (state legislation).

## Real estate licensing (DBPR, Chapter 475)
- Buying/selling **for our own account** needs no license.
- **Wholesaling:** Florida has no wholesaling-specific statute; assigning your own contract or double closing is generally treated as dealing in your own equitable interest, but marketing a property you don't own/control, or acting for others for compensation, risks **unlicensed brokerage (§475.42, a 3rd-degree felony)**. Recommend at least one **licensed agent/broker** on the team (also saves commissions and gives MLS access).
- Property management for **third parties** requires a broker license; managing our own properties does not.

## Contractor licensing (DBPR CILB) & owner-builder
- Use **state-certified or registered contractors** (general/residential, roofing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC). Verify on myfloridalicense.com.
- **Owner-builder exemption (F.S. 489.103(7)) does NOT apply** to properties built/improved for sale or lease within 1 year — flips and rentals need licensed contractors pulling permits.
- Unlicensed contracting is a crime and voids lien protections; get **Notice of Commencement** recorded for jobs > $5,000 (and > $15,000 for HVAC repair) and collect lien releases.

## Building permits
- **Boca Raton:** City Development Services (incorporated areas). **Delray Beach:** City Building Division. **Unincorporated:** Palm Beach County PZB (fees +2.7% CPI Mar 2026).
- Permits needed for: roof, windows/doors (must meet HVHZ-adjacent wind codes; Florida Building Code), electrical, plumbing, HVAC change-outs, structural, pools, fences, driveways, water heaters.
- **FEMA 50% / Substantial Improvement rule:** In Special Flood Hazard Areas (A/AE/VE zones), if cumulative improvement cost ≥ 50% of the structure's market value (excluding land), the house must be brought into full flood compliance — usually **elevation** — which can kill a flip. X zones are exempt. Boca Raton offers flood zone determinations; open permits count toward the cumulative total.
- **Historic districts (Delray):** Old School Square, Del-Ida Park, Marina, Nassau Park (+ individually designated sites) need a **Certificate of Appropriateness** for exterior work (staff or Historic Preservation Board review). Boca has designated historic properties (e.g., Old Floresta) with similar review.
- Unpermitted prior work: search permit history before purchase; plan for "after-the-fact" permits.

## Landlord–tenant law (Florida Statutes Chapter 83)
- **State preemption (HB 1417, 2023, §83.425):** local governments can't regulate landlord-tenant matters (notices, deposits, lease terms). Fair housing laws still apply.
- **Security deposits (§83.49):** hold in Florida bank account (interest/non-interest per disclosure); give notice of claim within **30 days** of move-out (refund within 15 days if no claim).
- **Notices:** 3-day notice for non-payment (excl. weekends/holidays); 7-day notice for non-compliance; month-to-month termination requires **30–60 days** notice.
- **Evictions** via county court; typical uncontested timeline 3–6 weeks.
- **Squatters:** HB 621 (2024) allows sheriff removal of unauthorized occupants by affidavit.
- **Fair Housing Act** (federal) + Florida Fair Housing Act + **Palm Beach County Fair Housing Ordinance** (adds protected classes, e.g., sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, age).
- **Lead paint disclosure** for pre-1978 homes; EPA **RRP certification** for renovators disturbing paint.
- Radon disclosure required in Florida leases and sales contracts.

## Short-term & seasonal rentals
- Rentals **≤ 6 months** are transient: collect **Florida sales tax (6% + 0.5% county surtax = 6.5%)** and **Palm Beach County Tourist Development Tax (6%)**; hosts must register/remit themselves (platforms do **not** remit TDT in PBC). Monthly returns due by the 20th.
- Rentals < 30 days and > 3 times/year require a **DBPR vacation rental license**.
- State law preempts local bans on vacation rentals (grandfathered rules pre-June 2011). ⚠️ Third-party sites claim Delray has a vacation rental registration program (Chapter 118, "DB-VR-" numbers) — unverified; confirm with city.
- HOAs/condos can still prohibit short-term rentals.
- Long-term residential leases (> 6 months) are **exempt** from sales tax.

## Condo-specific laws (if ever buying condos)
- **Milestone inspections** and **Structural Integrity Reserve Studies (SIRS)** for buildings with 3+ habitable stories; HB 913 (2025) extended deadlines and added reserve flexibility, but large special assessments remain common; Palm Beach had the lowest compliance (~28%) among tri-county areas.

## Insurance-related legal points
- §627.7011: insurers can't refuse/non-renew a **homeowner's** policy solely for roof age if roof < 15 years, or ≥ 15 years with inspection showing ≥ 5 years of life. Landlord (DP-3) policies were not clearly covered as of 2025; 2026 bills (SB 808) proposed extending it ⚠️ verify enacted status.
