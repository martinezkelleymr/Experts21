# 06 — Financing

*Rates as of mid/late 2026; quotes change weekly.*

## Options by strategy
| Product | Typical 2026 terms | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard money / fix-and-flip bridge** | 10–13% interest-only, 1.5–2.5 pts, 12-month term w/ balloon; up to 85–90% of purchase + 100% of rehab (draws), capped ~70–75% of ARV. FL 2025 median note rate 10.45% | Flips, BRRRR acquisition |
| **DSCR loan (30-yr)** | ~6.0–8.0% (promo from 5.75%); 20–25% down; DSCR ≥ 1.0–1.25; 660–700+ FICO; prepayment penalty (5-4-3-2-1 typical) | Long-term rentals, BRRRR refi |
| **Conventional investment mortgage** | Market rate + ~0.5–0.75%; 15–25% down; counts against personal DTI; max 10 financed properties | Early rentals bought by principals |
| **Portfolio / local bank & credit union** | Relationship-based; blanket loans, lines of credit | Scaling, cross-collateralized |
| **Private money (individuals)** | 8–12%, flexible; secured by 1st mortgage; must comply with securities laws if pooled | Gap/down payments |
| **Seller financing / subject-to** | Negotiated | Creative acquisitions; due-on-sale clause risk |
| **HELOC / business line of credit** | Prime-based | Down payments, rehab float |
| **Equity raise / fund (syndication)** | LP/GP structure | Scale — requires SEC Reg D (506(b)/506(c)) & securities counsel |
| **Transactional funding** | 1–2% for same-day double close | Wholesale double closings |

## Lender landscape
- National: Kiavi, LendingOne, RCN, Lima One, CoreVest, Griffin Funding, Ridge Street Capital, Visio.
- Local: South Florida private lenders & community banks (build 2–3 relationships; ask local title companies who funds most investor deals).

## Underwriting rules of thumb
- **DSCR** = rent ÷ PITIA (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA). At $600K price, 25% down, 7% rate: P&I ≈ $2,995 + taxes ~$950 + insurance ~$550 ≈ **$4,500/mo** → need ~$5,400 rent for 1.2x. Shows why rentals need below-market buys.
- Insurance quote **before** contract — it can kill a DSCR deal.
- Include Florida doc stamps on note (0.35%) + intangible tax (0.2%) in closing cost estimates.
- Recommended capital stack per flip: hard money + 10–20% equity + 10–15% contingency reserve.

## Capital strategy for year 1–3
1. Year 1: principals' equity + hard money for 3–6 flips; 1–3 BRRRRs.
2. Year 2: private-money investor list; DSCR refis build rental portfolio.
3. Year 3: consider a small 506(b) fund or bank portfolio line.
