# 03 — Competitive Landscape

## Who we compete with for deals
| Competitor type | Examples / notes | How they win | How we beat them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local "We Buy Houses" cash buyers & wholesalers** | ~80+ listed for Delray alone (ListWithClever); e.g., FL Cash Home Buyers LLC (800+ transactions), Golex Properties (Boca) | Heavy direct-mail/PPC, fast 7–14 day closes, cover closing costs | Hyper-local brand, faster underwriting, honest offers, referral network with probate/divorce attorneys |
| **Small/mid local flippers & builders** | Dominant buyer group in Lake Ida, Boca Square, Delray east side | Relationships with agents, in-house crews, tear-down/new build capability | Specialize in a few micro-markets, standardized renovation scopes, design that photographs well |
| **Individual "mom-and-pop" landlords** | Majority of the ~15% investor-owned SFH stock | Patient capital, lower return hurdle | Better systems/management; buy at scale off-market |
| **Institutional SFR operators** | Invitation Homes, Progress Residential, AMH, Tricon etc. — only ~3.8% of county SFH | Cheap capital, buy-box algorithms | They mostly avoid older east-side homes needing heavy rehab and HOA-heavy 55+ — that is our niche |
| **iBuyers / instant-offer platforms** | Opendoor & marketplaces (Clever Offers, HomeLight Simple Sale) | Convenience for sellers with move-in-ready homes | They avoid distressed property — we target it |
| **Owner-occupant retail buyers** | Especially post-Amendment 3 homestead relief | Pay full retail for move-in-ready | Compete on distressed deals they can't finance |
| **Luxury developers / spec builders** | East Boca & Delray coastal | Land value play, tear-down | Don't compete; sell lots/tear-downs to them when appropriate |

## Competitor for tenants
- New Class-A apartment complexes in downtown Boca/Delray and along Congress Ave — compete on amenities. SFR differentiator: yard, garage, school zone, pets.
- Seasonal furnished rentals compete with condos/hotels; Atlantic Ave proximity matters.

## Competitive intelligence tasks for agents
- Track monthly: investor purchases by LLC buyers in target ZIPs (Property Appraiser sales records), flips resold within 12 months (BrokerOne/ATTOM), new "we buy houses" ad spend (Google Ads auction insights).
- Maintain a list of top 20 active local flippers and their typical purchase/resale spreads — they are also potential **buyers for wholesale deals** and JV partners.
