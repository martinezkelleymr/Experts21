# 02 — Target Neighborhoods

*Neighborhood-level public data is thin; this is a starting map built from investor guides, market reports and local knowledge. Validate with MLS comps (sold, not list) before acting.*

## How to segment the area
| Zone | Character | Best fit strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **East of I-95, east of US-1 (coastal/Intracoastal)** | Waterfront, beach, $1.5M–$10M+ | Luxury flips / tear-downs only; flood-zone & 50% rule risk |
| **East of I-95, west of US-1** | 1950s–70s ranch/CBS homes, no HOA, walkable to downtowns | **Core flip & BRRRR zone** |
| **I-95 to Military Trail** | 1970s–80s subdivisions, some HOAs | Rentals, light flips |
| **West of Military / unincorporated** | Planned communities, HOAs, golf/country clubs, 55+ | Rentals where HOA allows; check lease restrictions |

## Screening criteria for the deal-finding agent
1. **Jurisdiction:** city vs. unincorporated (affects permits, code, BTR).
2. **Flood zone** (FEMA X vs. AE/VE) — use Boca Raton's flood zone service / FEMA map; AE zones trigger the 50% substantial-improvement rule.
3. **HOA / condo docs:** leasing restrictions (min term, cap on rentals, 1st-year no-lease rules), approval fees, special assessments, SIRS status.
4. **Historic designation** (Delray's four districts, Boca historic properties).
5. **Year built:** pre-1978 (lead paint), 1950s–70s (cast-iron drains, galvanized pipes, electrical panels such as FPE/Zinsco), 2001–2008 (Chinese drywall risk).
6. **School zones** for family rentals; distance to Atlantic Ave / Mizner Park / beach for flips.
7. **Roof age** (≥15 yrs complicates insurance) and **4-point inspection** for homes 25+ years old.
