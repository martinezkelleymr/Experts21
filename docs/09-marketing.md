# 09 — Marketing & Deal Sourcing

Two audiences: **sellers** (to buy below market) and **buyers/tenants** (to exit). Seller lead generation is the engine of the business.

## Seller lead sources (motivated / off-market)
| Channel | Est. cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct mail** to targeted lists | $0.60–$1.00/piece; 1–3 deals per 10K pieces | Lists: absentee owners, long-time owners (20+ yrs), probate, pre-foreclosure (lis pendens), tax delinquent, code violations, expired listings, tired landlords, inherited homes, divorce |
| **PPC (Google Ads "sell my house fast Boca Raton")** | $40–$150+/click; $300–$800/lead | Competitive — dozens of "we buy houses" firms |
| **SEO / local content** | $1K–$5K/mo | Neighborhood pages, Google Business Profile, reviews |
| **SMS / cold calling** | VA cost + data | Must comply with **TCPA**, National DNC, **Florida Telephone Solicitation Act (FTSA)** — FL has strict rules on autodialers & call times (8am–8pm); consult counsel |
| **Agent relationships / MLS** | Commission | Stale listings, "as-is" listings, pocket listings; agents bring 30–50% of deals in competitive markets |
| **Wholesalers** | Assignment fee | Build relationships, verify they have equitable interest |
| **Probate, estate & divorce attorneys, CPAs, financial planners** | Referral relationships | Big in an older demographic (Boca/Delray have large 65+ population) |
| **Foreclosure auctions** | Cash | Palm Beach County Clerk online auctions (realforeclose); tax deed sales |
| **Driving for dollars** | Time + app | DealMachine-type apps |
| **Bandit signs** | Cheap | Often prohibited by city codes — fines; avoid |

## Data tools
PropStream, BatchLeads/BatchData, ATTOM, PBC Property Appraiser (pbcpao.gov), PBC Clerk records (lis pendens, probate), city code enforcement records.

## Exit marketing
- **Flips:** List on MLS (Beaches MLS / Miami MLS via broker) with professional photos, drone, video; time listings for season; staging; highlight new roof/impact windows/insurance-friendly features (lower insurance is a selling point in 2026).
- **Rentals:** Zillow Rental Manager, Apartments.com, MLS rental listings, FAU and hospital housing boards; corporate relocation contacts.
- **Wholesale / JV:** Cash-buyer list (local flippers, builders); private email list; investor networking (BiggerPockets, local REIAs, e.g., Palm Beach / South Florida REIA groups).

## Brand & positioning
- Local, trustworthy, transparent "fair cash offer" brand; emphasize respect for long-time owners/seniors (avoid predatory tactics — Florida has elder-exploitation protections and reputational risk).
- Collect Google reviews from every seller and tenant.
- Showcase renovations (before/after) on Instagram/YouTube — builds both seller trust and buyer demand.

## KPIs to track
Cost per lead, cost per contract, lead→appointment→offer→contract conversion, deals per channel, average spread per deal, days to close, marketing ROI by channel.
