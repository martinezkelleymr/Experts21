# 08 — Staffing & Organization

## Phase 1 (0–12 months, 4–10 deals): lean core + outsourced
| Role | In-house / outsourced | Est. cost (South Florida) |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / CEO – capital & strategy | In-house | Equity |
| **Acquisitions manager** (lead follow-up, seller appointments, offers) | In-house | $50K base + $1K–$3K/deal commission (~$90K–$150K OTE) |
| **Project/construction manager** (scopes, bids, draws, permits) | In-house or contract | $75K–$110K or 8–12% of rehab budget |
| **Licensed real estate agent/broker** (MLS, listing flips, comps) | Partner or employee | Commission splits; or $60K–$90K + bonus |
| **Transaction coordinator** | Virtual/contract | $300–$500 per file |
| **Lead manager / cold caller / texting VA** | Offshore VA | $8–$15/hr |
| Bookkeeper | Contract | $500–$1,500/mo |
| CPA (real estate specialist) | Contract | $5K–$15K/yr |
| Real estate attorney | Retainer / per deal | $1,500–$3,000 per contract set; litigation hourly |
| Title company / closing agent | Vendor | Per transaction |
| **General contractor(s)** & licensed trades | Vendors (2–3 GCs bid each job) | Per job |
| **Property manager** (third-party, licensed) | Vendor | 8–12% of rent |
| Insurance broker (independent, Florida-admitted & surplus lines) | Vendor | Commission |

## Phase 2 (12–36 months, 15–30+ deals/yr): build in-house
- Add: Disposition manager (selling flips/wholesales to buyers list), second acquisitions rep, marketing manager, in-house PM/leasing coordinator (requires broker license if managing others' property), maintenance tech, controller.

## Key hiring/vendor criteria
- Florida licenses verified on **myfloridalicense.com** (contractors, agents, PMs, appraisers).
- Contractors: Certificate of insurance (GL + workers' comp), local permitting track record in Boca/Delray, references from other investors.
- Bilingual (English/Spanish/Portuguese/Haitian Creole) staff valuable for seller outreach and tenants.
- Use 1099 vs W-2 classification correctly (Florida & IRS tests).

## Where AI expert agents can substitute or augment
- Lead scoring & comps (acquisitions analyst), rehab estimate drafting, permit research, insurance quote comparison, tenant screening workflow, marketing copy, bookkeeping categorization, compliance checklists. Human sign-off remains essential for offers, contracts, and anything licensed.
