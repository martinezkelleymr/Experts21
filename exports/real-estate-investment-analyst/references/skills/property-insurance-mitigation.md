# Property insurance quoting & mitigation

## Purpose
Know the real insurance cost and insurability **before** the inspection period ends.

## Procedure
1. Get roof age, roof type, electrical panel, plumbing type, water heater age, AC age, windows/doors (impact or shutters), year built, and flood zone.
2. Order or review a **4-point inspection** (roof, electrical, plumbing, HVAC; typically required for homes 25+ years old) and a **wind-mitigation report** (roof shape, deck attachment, roof-to-wall connections, secondary water resistance, opening protection).
3. Quote the right product for each phase: **builder's risk/vacant** during rehab, **DP-3 landlord** for rentals, **HO-3** for the resale buyer (that buyer's cost affects ARV), **flood** (NFIP or private), **GL + umbrella** at the company level.
4. Use an independent broker with admitted and surplus-lines markets; Citizens is a last resort.
5. Model upgrades: impact roof, impact windows and doors, hip roof straps and secondary water barrier can each cut premiums materially (10–20%+).

## Law & market notes (verify current status)
- §627.7011: a homeowner's policy can't be refused or non-renewed solely for roof age if the roof is < 15 years old, or ≥ 15 years with an inspection showing ≥ 5 years of life left. Landlord (DP-3) coverage under this rule was unclear as of 2025; SB 808 (2026) proposed extending it.
- PBC average HO premium ≈ $6,300 (mid-2026, state data), trending slightly down; Citizens cut PBC rates ≈ 8–12% in 2026.
- Hurricane season Jun 1–Nov 30: binding restrictions apply when a named storm threatens, so bind early.

## Output
A coverage plan table (policy, carrier type, premium range, deductible incl. hurricane %, conditions), recommended mitigation upgrades with payback, and red flags (uninsurable items).
