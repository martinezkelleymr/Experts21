# 05 — Startup & Operating Costs

*All figures are planning estimates (2026). Confirm with vendors.*

## One-time startup costs (company level)
| Item | Estimate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Florida LLC formation (Sunbiz) | $125 | Articles of Organization + registered agent designation |
| Registered agent service | $0–$300/yr | Can self-serve with a FL address |
| Annual report (Sunbiz) | $138.75/yr | Due Jan 1–May 1; **$400 late fee** |
| Operating agreement / attorney setup | $1,500–$5,000 | Multi-member LLC, holding-company + series/property LLCs |
| CPA setup / entity tax planning | $1,000–$3,000 | S-corp election for flip entity vs. partnership for rentals |
| Local Business Tax Receipts (city + county) | ~$50–$300 each | Per business location/activity |
| Insurance — general liability, E&O, umbrella | $2,000–$6,000/yr | Plus builder's risk per rehab |
| Software stack (CRM, MLS/data, PM, accounting) | $300–$1,500/mo | e.g., PropStream/BatchLeads, REI CRM, AppFolio/Buildium, QuickBooks |
| Website, branding, call tracking | $3,000–$15,000 | |
| Initial marketing (direct mail, PPC) | $5,000–$20,000/mo | See 09-marketing.md |
| Working capital reserve | 6+ months of overhead | |
| **Capital per deal** | **$100K–$250K** down + rehab | Hard-money: 10–20% down + closing + reserves; rentals: 20–25% down |

## Acquisition closing costs (buyer side, Palm Beach County)
- Doc stamps on **mortgage note**: $0.35 per $100 of loan; **intangible tax** 0.2% of loan.
- Lender title policy, settlement fee, recording: ~1–2% total.
- Owner's title policy: customarily seller-paid in Palm Beach County (negotiable — confirm with title company).
- Deed doc stamps 0.70% — customarily **seller-paid** (affects us when we sell flips).
- Inspections: general, 4-point, wind mitigation, WDO (termite), sewer scope, roof — ~$800–$1,500.
- Lender points: hard money 1.5–2.5 pts; DSCR 0–2 pts.

## Renovation cost benchmarks (South Florida 2026)
| Item | Range |
|---|---|
| Light cosmetic rehab | $25–$45/sq ft |
| Mid-level rehab (kitchen, baths, flooring, paint) | $60–$110/sq ft |
| Full gut / systems | $120–$200+/sq ft |
| Kitchen (mid-range, total) | $25K–$60K (Delray guide: $350–$500/sq ft of kitchen area) |
| Roof replacement 1,700–2,000 sf | $18K–$32K (impact-rated $22K–$36K) |
| Impact windows (whole house) | $20K–$45K |
| Labor rates | Carpenter $85–$130/hr; plumber $120–$180/hr |
| Permits | ~1–3% of construction value + plan review; Palm Beach County PZB fees +2.7% CPI (Mar 2026) |
| Contingency | 10–15% minimum (20% for pre-1970 homes) |

## Ongoing operating costs — rental property (per house, annual)
| Item | Estimate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Property tax | ~1.7–2.0% of purchase price | Boca millage ~3.65 city + county 4.5 + school, etc. (total ~17–19 mills); Delray city ~6.16 (total ~19–21 mills). Non-homestead cap drops to **5%/yr from 2027** (Amendment 3) |
| Homeowners/landlord (DP-3) insurance | $4,000–$9,000+ | PBC average HO premium ~$6,300 (state data, mid-2026), trending slightly down; impact roof/windows cut cost 10–20%+ |
| Flood insurance | $600–$5,000+ | Required by lenders in AE zones; recommended everywhere |
| HOA dues | $0–$6,000+ | |
| Property management | 8–12% of collected rent (some 5–9%) | + leasing fee ½–1 month rent; renewal ~$200–$250 |
| Maintenance & repairs | 5–8% of rent | Older homes higher; AC is the big one |
| Capex reserve | 5–10% of rent | Roof, AC, water heater |
| Vacancy | 4–8% | |
| Lawn/pool | $1,200–$3,000 | Often tenant-paid lawn; pool service usually landlord |
| Pest/termite bond | $300–$800 | |

## Company overhead (lean year-1 team)
- Salaries/contractors: see 08-staffing.md (≈ $250K–$600K/yr depending on in-house vs. outsourced).
- Office: virtual/coworking $300–$1,500/mo, or home office.
- Legal/accounting: $10K–$30K/yr.
