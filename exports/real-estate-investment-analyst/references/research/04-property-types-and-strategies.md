# 04 — Property Types & Investment Strategies

The company runs three strategies. Each property should be underwritten for **all three exits** and assigned to the best one.

## Strategy A — Buy & Hold Rental (recurring income)
- **Best property types:** 3/2 and 4/2 single-family CBS (concrete block) homes, 1970s–2000s, no or low HOA, west of I-95 near A-rated schools; duplexes/small multifamily (2–4 units) east of I-95 in Delray where zoning allows.
- **Target metrics:** DSCR ≥ 1.20, cash-on-cash ≥ 6–8%, cap rate ≥ 5.5% (hard in this market — see 01-local-market.md). Expect appreciation + amortization to carry total return.
- **Lease type:** 12-month unfurnished leases (sales-tax exempt when > 6 months). Option: furnished **seasonal** (3–6 month) rentals in winter at premium rates — but those are **transient rentals** subject to 6% county TDT + state sales tax and may need a DBPR vacation-rental license (see 07).
- **Avoid:** 55+ communities, condos with leasing caps or pending SIRS special assessments, HOAs with 1–2-year "no lease" rules.

## Strategy B — Fix & Flip (renovate and resell)
- **Best property types:** Dated 1950s–80s ranch homes east of I-95 (Boca Square, Lake Ida, Boca Villas, Delray Shores, Rainberry Bay); estate/probate homes; homes with failed insurance inspections (old roof, panel) that retail buyers can't finance.
- **Underwriting rule:** Max offer ≈ **70–75% of ARV − rehab cost** (adjust down for long days-on-market; current SFH ~42–78 days).
- **Holding cost reality:** 6–9 month cycle (permits 1–3 months + rehab 3–4 + sale 2). Budget ~1–1.5% of purchase price per month carrying (hard-money interest, insurance, taxes, utilities).
- **Exit timing:** List in **Nov–Apr** (season) when buyer traffic peaks.
- **Selling costs:** ~5–6% commissions + ~1% closing; seller customarily pays deed doc stamps (0.70%).

## Strategy C — BRRRR (renovate and rent)
- Buy distressed → renovate → rent → **cash-out refinance with DSCR loan** (typically 70–75% LTV of new appraised value) → repeat.
- Best where ARV uplift is large relative to cost (cosmetic + systems homes), rents are strong (near FAU, hospital, downtowns).
- Watch **seasoning requirements** (many DSCR lenders require 3–6 months of ownership before using new appraised value).

## Property-type cheat sheet
| Type | Pros | Cons | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| SFH, no HOA | Freedom to rent/renovate, best resale | Highest price per door | A, B, C |
| SFH in HOA | Amenities, schools | Lease approval, fees, rules | A (if lease-friendly) |
| Townhome / villa | Lower entry price | HOA, roof/insurance shared | A |
| Condo (3+ habitable stories) | Cheapest entry | SIRS/milestone special assessments, condo insurance, leasing caps, financing hard ("non-warrantable") | Generally avoid |
| 55+ condo/villa | Very cheap | Age restriction limits tenant pool & resale | Avoid |
| Duplex / 2–4 unit | Better yield | Limited supply, zoning | A, C |
| Waterfront / coastal | Highest ARV, luxury buyers | Flood zone, 50% rule, elevation, insurance | B only (experienced) |
| Tear-down lot | Land play | Builder competition, entitlements | Wholesale to builders |

## Common South Florida rehab scope items
Roof (impact-rated, adds insurance credit), impact windows/doors (wind-mitigation discount), electrical panel replacement, cast-iron drain lining/replacement, re-pipe (galvanized/polybutylene), AC/ductwork, kitchen & baths, flooring (tile/LVP), exterior paint, pool resurfacing, landscaping, hurricane shutters.
