# 10 — Biggest Risks & Mitigations

| # | Risk | Why it matters here | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Property insurance cost & availability** | PBC avg HO premium ~$6.3K (2026), higher for older homes; carriers exit/non-renew; roofs ≥15 yrs & old panels make homes uninsurable on standard markets | Get binding quote before contract end of inspection period; prioritize impact roof/windows; wind mitigation report; use independent broker; budget conservatively |
| 2 | **Hurricanes & flood** | Season Jun 1–Nov 30; coastal & AE zones; storm surge; rising flood insurance (NFIP Risk Rating 2.0) | Avoid high-risk flood zones for rentals; flood insurance everywhere; hurricane prep plan for vacant rehabs; builder's risk; reserves |
| 3 | **FEMA 50% rule** | Major rehab in flood zone can force elevation | Check flood zone & structure value before offer; scope to stay under 50% or price in elevation |
| 4 | **Thin rental yields / negative cash flow** | Rents ~flat; prices high; taxes + insurance high | Buy only at strong discount; BRRRR; higher down payment; target lower-tax/no-HOA homes; stress-test DSCR at +20% insurance |
| 5 | **Market softening / overvaluation** | 8+ months supply in early 2026 (Redfin), condo weakness, investor pullback statewide | Underwrite conservative ARV (sold comps ≤ 6 months), shorter projects, multiple exits (sell, rent, refi) |
| 6 | **Interest rate & refinance risk** | Hard money 10–13%; BRRRR depends on refi | Fixed-rate DSCR; avoid balloon dependency; cap holding periods |
| 7 | **Rehab overruns & contractor issues** | Older CBS homes hide cast-iron, electrical, termite, mold issues; labor costs high; contractor fraud | Thorough inspections (sewer scope, 4-point); 10–20% contingency; licensed contractors; draw schedules tied to inspections; lien releases |
| 8 | **Permit delays** | Boca/Delray review times; historic reviews; unpermitted prior work | Pre-offer permit search; expediter; factor 1–3 months |
| 9 | **Property tax reassessment** | Tax resets to market on purchase; local millage may rise after Amendment 3 | Underwrite on post-sale assessed value; cap growth now 5% (from 2027) |
| 10 | **HOA/condo restrictions & special assessments** | Leasing bans, approvals, SIRS assessments ($10Ks per unit) | Read docs pre-contract; estoppel letter; avoid older condos |
| 11 | **Regulatory/legal** | Unlicensed brokerage (wholesaling), TCPA/FTSA telemarketing suits, fair housing complaints, STR tax non-compliance | Licensed broker on team; compliance counsel; fair-housing training; TDT/sales tax registration |
| 12 | **Tenant risk** | Non-payment, damage, eviction time | Screening (credit, income 3x rent, eviction & criminal per HUD guidance), deposits, rent guarantee insurance, professional PM |
| 13 | **Concentration risk** | Two adjacent cities, one hurricane zone | Diversify by property type & sub-market; strong reserves; consider adjacent markets (Boynton, Deerfield, Lake Worth) |
| 14 | **Competition for deals** | Many cash buyers, ~20% of sales to investors | Niche focus, speed, relationships, data-driven lead lists |
| 15 | **Key-person / capital risk** | Small team, reliance on a few lenders | Multiple lender relationships, documented processes (expert agents), cross-training |
| 16 | **Environmental/hidden defects** | Chinese drywall (2001–08 builds), polybutylene, lead paint pre-1978, mold, termites | Specialized inspections; disclosures; RRP-certified crews |
