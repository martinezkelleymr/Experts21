# Regulatory & legislative monitoring

*Roster skill S27. Used by: `compliance-officer` (owner), `real-estate-attorney`, `landlord-tenant-counsel`, `tax-strategist`, `insurance-risk-advisor`, `market-research-analyst`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Make sure team advice reflects current law, and spot changes early.

## Watch list (as of Nov 2026)
- **Amendment 3** (passed Nov 3, 2026): homestead exemption to $150K (2027) / $250K (2028); non-homestead cap 10% → 5% from Jan 1, 2027; watch implementing legislation and local millage responses.
- **Insurance:** §627.7011 roof-age rules; SB 808 / HB 815 / SB 128 (2026) on roof age and inspections; Citizens rate changes; flood mandates for Citizens policyholders.
- **Condo:** HB 913 (2025) milestone and SIRS changes; future reserve rules.
- **Landlord-tenant:** Chapter 83; §83.425 preemption; squatter law (HB 621).
- **STR:** state preemption; PBC TDT; any city registration programs.
- **Licensing and marketing:** Chapter 475 (wholesaling bills); TCPA and Florida FTSA amendments; FinCEN real estate reporting.
- **Local:** Boca Raton and Delray Beach city commission agendas (zoning, historic, permit fees), PBC PZB fee changes (+2.7% CPI Mar 2026).

## Procedure
1. Search official sources first (flsenate.gov, myfloridahouse.gov, floir.com, city and county sites), then reputable news.
2. For each item: status (filed / passed / signed / effective date), who it affects, impact on our three strategies, action needed.
3. Mark anything uncertain as **unverified** and name the office that can confirm it.
4. Propose doc updates for `docs/07-permits-licenses-and-legal.md` (the team architect applies them).

## Output
A dated regulatory bulletin table: item, status, effective date, impact, action, source.
