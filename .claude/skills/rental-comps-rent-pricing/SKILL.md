# Rental comps & rent pricing

*Roster skill S02. Used by: `market-research-analyst` (owner), `underwriting-analyst`, `property-manager`, `chief-investment-strategist`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Estimate achievable rent and days-to-lease so rental and BRRRR underwriting rests on real comps.

## Procedure
1. Gather **leased** comps (MLS rental closings, property-manager data) from the last 6 months; use active listings only as a ceiling.
2. Match on beds/baths, sq ft, school zone, HOA, pool, garage, pet policy and condition.
3. Separate **annual unfurnished** (12-month) from **seasonal furnished** (Nov–Apr, ≤ 6 months) pricing. Seasonal rentals are transient: they need county TDT plus sales tax registration, may need a DBPR license, and often have HOA limits.
4. Give rent as a range plus expected days on market. Flag concessions.
5. Apply vacancy (4–8%) and say whether the rent supports DSCR ≥ 1.20 at the target price (hand the numbers to `deal-underwriting-proforma`).

## Local benchmarks (2026, verify)
- Boca houses avg ≈ $5,500–$5,700/mo; 3BR ≈ $4,800; 4BR ≈ $6,000; rents roughly flat (+0.5% YoY).
- Delray apartments ≈ $2,450 (1BR) to ≈ $2,950 avg; SFH rents flat to slightly down.
- Demand anchors: FAU, Boca Regional Hospital, Office Depot/IBM corridor, A-rated schools in west Boca, downtown Delray/Atlantic Ave.

## Output
Rent range (annual and, if relevant, seasonal), comp table, days to lease, confidence, and risks (HOA lease caps, seasonality).
