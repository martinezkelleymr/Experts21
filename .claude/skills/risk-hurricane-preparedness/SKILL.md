# Risk assessment & hurricane preparedness

*Roster skill S34. Used by: `insurance-risk-advisor` (owner), `operations-director`, `renovation-project-manager`, `property-manager`, `chief-investment-strategist`, `real-estate-investment-analyst`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
See risks early, put owners and mitigations on each one, and survive storm season.

## Risk register (from docs/10)
Insurance cost/availability · hurricanes and flood · FEMA 50% rule · thin rental yields · market softening · rates/refinance · rehab overruns · permit delays · property-tax reassessment · HOA/condo assessments · regulatory (Ch. 475, TCPA/FTSA, fair housing, STR taxes) · tenant default · geographic concentration · competition · key-person/capital · hidden defects. Score each by likelihood × impact (1–5) with an owner, mitigation and trigger.

## Hurricane plan (Jun 1–Nov 30)
- **Before season (May):** confirm policies and hurricane deductibles; photo and video every property; roof and gutter checks; shutters or panels on site; vendor standby agreements; cash reserve for deductibles.
- **Watch/warning:** secure vacant rehabs (materials, dumpsters, open openings), install shutters, sandbags in low areas, pause deliveries; notify tenants with the prep checklist; know that insurers stop binding new coverage once a storm threatens.
- **After:** safety first; document damage before cleanup; mitigate further damage (tarps, water extraction) and keep receipts; file claims fast; check for substantial-damage determinations in flood zones.

## Output
An updated risk register table (risk, score, owner, mitigation, trigger, status) or a storm action checklist with assignments.
