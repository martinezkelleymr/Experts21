# Transaction coordination & closing management

*Roster skill S33. Used by: `transaction-coordinator` (owner), `real-estate-attorney`, `acquisitions-specialist`, `dispositions-specialist`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Close every deal on time, with no missed deadlines or wire mistakes.

## Critical-date calendar (build on day 0)
Effective date · escrow deposit due (often 3 days) · inspection-period end · loan application and commitment · appraisal · title commitment and objection deadline · survey · HOA/condo application and approval · insurance bound (before closing) · final walkthrough · closing · post-closing (utilities, BTR, PM handoff).

## Checklist
- Open title with our approved title company; order the municipal lien search (open permits, code liens) and the estoppel.
- Track inspection, 4-point, wind-mit, sewer scope and insurance quotes (feeds the go/no-go before the inspection deadline).
- Lender: conditions list, appraisal, draw setup (rehab loans).
- Review the closing statement against the contract: prorations (taxes, HOA), doc stamps, credits, payoff figures.
- **Wire safety:** verify wiring instructions by phone using a known number; never accept changed instructions by email (`cybersecurity-wire-fraud-prevention`).
- Post-closing: record the deed, store documents, update the CRM, the accounting system and the insurance schedule.

## Output
A deal timeline table (task, owner, due date, status), a risk flags list and a closing-statement review.
