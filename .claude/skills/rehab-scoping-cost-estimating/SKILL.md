# Rehab scoping & cost estimating

*Roster skill S11. Used by: `renovation-project-manager` (owner), `underwriting-analyst`, `due-diligence-specialist`, `interior-design-staging-specialist`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Produce a realistic scope and budget that matches the exit (a rental needs durability; a flip needs buyer appeal).

## Benchmarks (2026, South Florida — verify with bids)
- Light cosmetic $25–$45/sq ft · mid-level $60–$110/sq ft · full gut/systems $120–$200+/sq ft.
- Kitchen (mid) $25K–$60K · bath $12K–$30K · roof $18K–$32K (impact $22K–$36K) · impact windows $20K–$45K · HVAC $8K–$15K · panel $2.5K–$5K · re-pipe $6K–$15K · cast-iron $8K–$25K · exterior paint $5K–$10K · pool resurface $8K–$20K · flooring (tile/LVP) $5–$12/sq ft installed.
- Labor: carpenter $85–$130/hr; plumber $120–$180/hr.
- Permits ≈ 1–3% of construction value; PBC fees +2.7% CPI (Mar 2026).
- Contingency 10–15% (20% for pre-1970 homes).

## Procedure
1. Walk room by room plus systems (roof, MEP, windows, exterior, site, pool).
2. For each line: quantity, unit cost, total, permit needed (Y/N), trade, finish level.
3. Prioritize: (a) safety, insurability and lender items, (b) value-adding items (kitchen, baths, curb appeal, impact windows), (c) optional items.
4. Check the total against the FEMA 50% threshold in flood zones (`flood-zone-fema-50-rule`).
5. Build a timeline: permits 1–3 months; rehab 6–16 weeks by scope.

## Output
A scope-of-work table with subtotals by trade, contingency, total, $/sq ft, permit list, timeline, and the ARV items vs. the maintenance-only items.
