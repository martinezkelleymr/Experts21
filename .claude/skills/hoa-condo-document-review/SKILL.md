# HOA & condo document review

*Roster skill S10. Used by: `due-diligence-specialist` (owner), `real-estate-attorney`, `underwriting-analyst`, `property-manager`, `real-estate-investment-analyst`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Make sure the association lets us do what the strategy needs, at a known cost.

## Documents to request
Declaration/CC&Rs, bylaws, rules and regulations, amendments, current budget, reserve study/**SIRS**, **milestone inspection** report (condos 3+ habitable stories), board minutes (12–24 months), estoppel certificate, insurance certificate, lease application and approval form, litigation disclosure.

## Investor red flags
- No leasing in the first 1–2 years of ownership; rental caps; minimum lease 6–12 months; maximum leases per year.
- Board approval of tenants with long timelines or high fees; interviews.
- **55+ age restriction** (shrinks the tenant and buyer pool).
- Pending or likely **special assessments** (SIRS-driven, roof, concrete restoration); underfunded reserves; HB 913 (2025) reserve pauses can be hiding problems.
- Litigation, insurance gaps, high delinquency rate (> 10%).
- Short-term rentals banned (associations can do this even though local governments can't).
- Architectural review needed for exterior renovations.

## Procedure
1. Extract every leasing, renovation and age rule and quote the section.
2. Summarize finances: dues, reserves %, upcoming assessments, delinquencies.
3. Score fit for each strategy: rental / flip / BRRRR / seasonal.
4. Note statutory buyer rights (Florida condo/HOA disclosure and rescission periods); the attorney confirms.

## Output
A fit memo: rules table with citations, financial summary, strategy fit scores, deal-breakers, questions for the manager.
