# Cybersecurity & wire-fraud prevention

*Roster skill S38. Used by: `proptech-architect` (owner), `transaction-coordinator`, `cfo-financial-planner`, `real-estate-attorney`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Never lose a closing's funds or a tenant's personal data.

## Wire rules (non-negotiable)
1. Get wiring instructions only from the verified title company, through a secure portal or in person.
2. **Call back** on a phone number from an independent source (not the email) to verify the account details before every wire; repeat for any change.
3. Treat any "updated instructions" email as fraud until proven otherwise.
4. Two-person approval for wires over a set amount; confirm receipt with the title company right after sending.
5. If fraud is suspected: call the bank immediately (recall), file at ic3.gov, notify the title company and the attorney.

## Baseline controls
MFA on email, banking, CRM, MLS and PM software; password manager; least-privilege access with offboarding checklists; encrypted storage for IDs, SSNs and bank info (tenant applications, investor docs); no personal data in unsecured spreadsheets or email; phishing training; device updates; backups.

## Output
A verification checklist for the transaction at hand, or a security assessment with prioritized fixes.
