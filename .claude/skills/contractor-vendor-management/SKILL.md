# Contractor & vendor management

*Roster skill S15. Used by: `renovation-project-manager` (owner), `operations-director`, `property-manager`, `transaction-coordinator`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Build a dependable bench of licensed, insured vendors with clear contracts.

## Vetting checklist
- **License** verified on myfloridalicense.com (the right category: certified/registered general, residential, roofing, plumbing, electrical, mechanical; real estate broker for property managers).
- Certificates of insurance: general liability, workers' comp (or valid exemption), auto; us named as additional insured.
- 3+ references from investors; local permitting track record in Boca, Delray and PBC; complaint and lien search.
- Capacity (crews, current jobs), communication, bilingual capability if useful.

## Bidding
Use the same written scope for 2–3 bids; compare line by line; reject vague lump sums; check unit prices against `rehab-scoping-cost-estimating`.

## Contract essentials
Scope, price, milestone payment schedule, retainage (5–10%), start and finish dates with remedies, change-order process, permits pulled by the contractor, lien waivers per draw, warranty, cleanup, insurance, termination rights, the Florida Construction Lien Law notice.

## Scorecard (quarterly)
On-time %, budget variance, quality and rework, inspection pass rate, responsiveness, safety. Keep notes per vendor.

## Output
A vendor shortlist or scorecard table and a recommendation, with the contract terms to negotiate.
