# Tenant screening, leasing & rental operations

*Roster skill S32. Used by: `property-manager` (owner), `landlord-tenant-counsel`, `operations-director`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Keep homes leased to qualified tenants, with low turnover and costs under control.

## Lifecycle
1. **Rent-ready:** safety items, smoke/CO detectors, clean AC filters, pest treatment, photos.
2. **Market:** price from `rental-comps-rent-pricing`; syndicate listings; self-showing or agent showings.
3. **Screen (written, uniform):** ID; income ≈ 3× rent (verified); credit; rental history; eviction records; criminal history with individualized assessment; pets and assistance-animal rules per `fair-housing-compliance`. HOA approval if required.
4. **Lease:** Florida-compliant lease (Ch. 83 disclosures: radon, lead paint pre-1978, deposit notice); HOA rules addendum; hurricane-prep addendum; maintenance responsibilities (lawn, filters, pool).
5. **Move-in:** dated photo and video inspection signed by the tenant.
6. **Operate:** online rent; late fee per lease; 3-day notice process; maintenance SLA (emergency 24h, AC failure same or next day in summer); preventive maintenance (AC service, pest, gutters, roof check before hurricane season).
7. **Renew:** offer 60–90 days out; market-based increase; month-to-month notice 30–60 days.
8. **Move-out:** inspection; deposit return or claim notice within Ch. 83 deadlines.

## Third-party PM oversight
Fees 8–12% of collected rent (some 5–9%), leasing ½–1 month, renewal ≈ $200–$250; check that the PM is a licensed broker; review the monthly owner statement, vacancy days, maintenance markup and response times.

## KPIs
Occupancy, days vacant, delinquency %, maintenance cost/door, renewal rate, tenant satisfaction.

## Output
A leasing or operations plan, a policy, or a PM scorecard, with deadlines and owners.
