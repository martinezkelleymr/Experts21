# Construction project & draw management

*Roster skill S14. Used by: `renovation-project-manager` (owner), `capital-markets-advisor`, `operations-director`, `cfo-financial-planner`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Deliver the rehab on time and on budget, and keep lender draws and lien protection clean.

## Procedure
1. **Baseline:** approved scope, budget by trade, schedule (permits → demo → rough-ins → inspections → close-up → finishes → punch → final), payment schedule tied to milestones (never pay well ahead of work).
2. **Weekly:** update the budget vs. actual and % complete, the critical path, and risks (weather, inspections, materials); post photos.
3. **Draws:** match the lender's draw schedule; include invoices, photos, inspection results and **conditional lien waivers**; after funding, collect **unconditional waivers** from the GC, subs and suppliers who served notices to owner.
4. **Change orders:** written, priced, approved by the project manager and CFO above a set amount; recheck the FEMA 50% total in flood zones.
5. **Hurricane protocol** (Jun–Nov): secure materials, shutters, photos for claims (see `risk-hurricane-preparedness`).
6. **Close-out:** final inspections, permit close, punch list, warranties and manuals, final unconditional waivers.

## KPIs
Schedule variance (days), cost variance (%), change orders as % of budget, rework items, days from purchase to listing or rental.

## Output
A status report: % complete, budget table, schedule, upcoming draw, risks and decisions needed.
