# Market & neighborhood research

*Roster skill S05. Used by: `market-research-analyst` (owner), `chief-investment-strategist`, `acquisitions-specialist`, `underwriting-analyst`, `data-analytics-engineer`, `real-estate-investment-analyst`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Keep a current, sourced view of the market at city, ZIP and neighborhood level.

## Procedure
1. Start from `docs/01-local-market.md` and `docs/02-target-neighborhoods.md` (snapshot Nov 2026). State the as-of date of any figure you use.
2. Refresh with current data (web search, MLS reports, Redfin/Zillow/Realtor associations, Palm Beach County Property Appraiser, Florida Realtors, MIAMI REALTORS county reports). Always say whether a figure is **SFH or all types**, **median sale or typical value**, and **city or mailing address**.
3. For a neighborhood, cover: price band, $/sq ft, rents, days to contract, months of supply, HOA presence, flood zone, school ratings, historic status, flip activity, investor share and key amenities (beach, Atlantic Ave, Mizner Park, Brightline/Tri-Rail).
4. Separate facts from interpretation; give the bull and bear case.

## Key context (Nov 2026)
- Boca typical value ≈ $577K (+3.7% YoY); Delray all-types median ≈ $360K–$390K, SFH ≈ $700K.
- Redfin early 2026: 8+ months supply (buyer's market) all types; county SFH ≈ 3.9 months.
- Investors ≈ 20% of PBC purchases; institutions own only ≈ 3.8% of SFH.
- Amendment 3 passed: non-homestead cap 10% → 5% from 2027; bigger homestead exemptions.
- Target zones: Boca Square, Boca Villas, Central Boca, Lake Ida, Delray Shores, Rainberry Bay, Osceola Park; avoid 55+ condo areas for rentals.

## Output
A market brief: headline metrics table (with sources and dates), trends, neighborhood scorecard, implications for each strategy, open questions.
