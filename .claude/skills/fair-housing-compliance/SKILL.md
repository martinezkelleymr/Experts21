# Fair housing compliance

*Roster skill S25. Used by: `landlord-tenant-counsel` (owner), `property-manager`, `compliance-officer`, `brand-content-marketer`, `lead-generation-marketer`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Treat every applicant and customer equally, and keep the company free of discrimination claims.

## Protected classes
- **Federal:** race, color, religion, sex (incl. sexual orientation and gender identity per HUD guidance), national origin, familial status, disability.
- **Florida:** same as federal (plus pregnancy under some provisions).
- **Palm Beach County ordinance:** adds classes such as marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression and age (verify the current ordinance).

## Rules of thumb
- Ads describe the **property**, not the ideal tenant ("great for families," "perfect for singles," "no kids," "mature person" are problems). Avoid "walking distance to church."
- Written, uniform **screening criteria** applied to everyone (income ≥ ~3× rent, credit, rental history, eviction records). Criminal history: individualized assessment, no blanket bans (HUD guidance); consider only relevant, recent convictions.
- **Reasonable accommodations and modifications:** assistance animals aren't pets (no pet rent or deposit); Florida law (§760.27) limits documentation demands and penalizes fraudulent ESA claims.
- 55+ communities are lawful exemptions only if they meet HOPA requirements.
- Keep records of all applicants and decisions.

## Output
A compliance review with issues, a risk level and compliant rewrites or procedures.
