# Entity structuring & securities

*Roster skill S21. Used by: `entity-securities-counsel` (owner), `cfo-financial-planner`, `tax-strategist`, `investor-relations-manager`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Isolate liability, keep the tax treatment clean and raise capital legally.

## Typical structure
```
Holding LLC (owners)
 ├── OpCo / Flip LLC (S-corp election) — acquisitions, rehab, staff, marketing
 ├── Rental Holdings LLC(s) — one per property or per small group (lender-dependent)
 └── Fund/JV LLCs — when raising outside equity
```
- Florida LLC via Sunbiz: $125 filing; annual report $138.75 due Jan 1–May 1 ($400 late fee); registered agent required.
- Operating agreements: capital calls, distributions waterfall, management rights, transfer restrictions, buy-sell, dispute resolution.
- Separate bank accounts and no commingling (protects the liability shield).
- Deeds into LLCs can trigger doc stamps and due-on-sale clauses; check with the attorney and lender.

## Reporting
- Check the current status of federal beneficial-ownership (CTA) reporting.
- FinCEN residential real estate rule: entity all-cash purchases may be reported by the closing or settlement agent; be ready to provide beneficial-owner information.

## Raising money
- Pooling passive investors' money for a share of profits is usually a **security**. Common paths: **Rule 506(b)** (no general solicitation; accredited investors plus up to 35 sophisticated investors) or **506(c)** (advertising allowed; accredited only, verified). File Form D and Florida notice filings.
- Single private-lender notes secured by a recorded mortgage are simpler but still need counsel.

## Output
A structure diagram, filing checklist, agreement term sheet and compliance notes. **Requires review by a licensed Florida attorney and the CPA.**
