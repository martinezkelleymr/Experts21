# Marketing compliance (TCPA / FTSA / DNC)

*Roster skill S28. Used by: `compliance-officer` (owner), `lead-generation-marketer`, `brand-content-marketer`, `proptech-architect`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Prevent telemarketing lawsuits (statutory damages per call or text add up fast) and protect the brand.

## Key requirements (verify current rules with counsel)
- **Consent:** autodialed or prerecorded calls and texts to cell phones need prior express (written, for marketing) consent under the TCPA; the FTSA has its own rules on automated systems.
- **Do Not Call:** scrub against the National DNC and Florida DNC lists; keep an internal DNC list; honor opt-outs immediately ("STOP").
- **Calling hours:** Florida limits telephone solicitation to **8 a.m.–8 p.m.** in the recipient's time zone, with a cap on calls about the same subject within 24 hours (check the current FTSA).
- **Identification:** give the caller's name and company; transmit a valid caller ID.
- **SMS:** register 10DLC campaigns; include opt-out language.
- **Email (CAN-SPAM):** accurate headers, a physical address, unsubscribe honored within 10 business days.
- **Direct mail:** no fake "official" or government-looking notices; no misleading urgency.
- **Elderly and vulnerable sellers:** no pressure; encourage family or attorney involvement.

## Procedure
Review the channel, list source, consent records, tool (dialer type), scripts and opt-out handling → classify risk → required fixes.

## Output
A compliance checklist with pass/fail by item, a risk rating, required changes and approved script language. **Counsel must approve the dialer and texting setup.**
