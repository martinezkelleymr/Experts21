# Relationship & network management

*Roster skill S31. Used by: `investor-relations-manager` (owner), `dispositions-specialist`, `acquisitions-specialist`, `capital-markets-advisor`, `lead-generation-marketer`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Turn relationships into a repeatable source of deals, buyers and capital.

## Relationship map
| Group | What they give | What we give |
|---|---|---|
| Private lenders/JV | Capital | Secured returns, reporting, track record |
| Cash buyers/builders | Exit for wholesale or tear-down deals | Vetted deals, clean contracts, fast closes |
| Listing agents | Off-market and "as-is" listings | Quick certain closes, repeat business, listing our flips with them |
| Wholesalers | Deals | Fast decisions, reliable closings |
| Probate/estate/divorce attorneys, CPAs, planners | Referrals of clients needing a sale | Fair, fast, documented offers; client-first approach |
| Title, lenders, PMs, contractors | Execution | Volume and loyalty |

## Procedure
1. Keep everything in the CRM: contact, category, last touch, deals sourced or funded, notes.
2. Cadence: A-list monthly, B-list quarterly; add value (market updates, deal reviews), not only asks.
3. Investor communications: offering-compliant (see `entity-structuring-securities`); no general solicitation of securities under 506(b).
4. Referral fees only to licensees where required (Ch. 475).
5. Quarterly review: which relationships produced deals, buyers or capital.

## Output
A relationship plan or pipeline table (contact type, goal, next action, date), outreach drafts and a review of results.
