# Real estate licensing compliance (Ch. 475)

*Roster skill S23. Used by: `compliance-officer` (owner), `real-estate-attorney`, `acquisitions-specialist`, `dispositions-specialist`, `lead-generation-marketer`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Avoid unlicensed brokerage, which is a third-degree felony under §475.42, while keeping deal flow efficient.

## Generally OK without a license (confirm with counsel)
- Buying, selling or leasing property **we own** (as owner/principal), through our own employees acting for the company.
- Assigning a purchase contract **we hold**, if marketed as an assignment of our contract interest with our role disclosed.

## Needs a license or broker involvement
- Marketing or "selling" a property we don't own or have under contract, or acting for others for compensation (including finder or referral fees to unlicensed people).
- Managing or leasing **other people's** properties for a fee.
- Paying commissions or bonuses to unlicensed people tied to closing a transaction (salaried employees working for the owner are different).

## Recommended controls
- Keep a licensed broker or agent on the team (also gives MLS access and listing ability).
- Write wholesale marketing scripts that say "we are assigning our contract interest."
- Keep a written compensation policy; route referral payments through licensees only.
- Train acquisitions and dispositions staff; keep a compliance log.

## Output
A compliance assessment of the proposed activity (OK / needs licensee / prohibited), required controls and approved wording. **A Florida real estate attorney must confirm edge cases.**
