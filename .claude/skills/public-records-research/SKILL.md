# Public records research

*Roster skill S06. Used by: `due-diligence-specialist` (owner), `acquisitions-specialist`, `real-estate-attorney`, `permitting-code-specialist`, `data-analytics-engineer`, `transaction-coordinator`, `real-estate-investment-analyst`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Find what the public record says about a property before money or effort is committed.

## Sources
- **Palm Beach County Property Appraiser** (pbcpao.gov): owner, mailing address (absentee?), homestead, assessed vs. market value, sales history, building sq ft, year built, **municipality** (city vs. unincorporated).
- **Clerk & Comptroller** (mypalmbeachclerk.com): recorded deeds, mortgages, satisfactions, liens (mechanic's, HOA, code), lis pendens (foreclosure), judgments, probate and guardianship cases, notices of commencement.
- **Tax Collector** (pbctax.gov): delinquent taxes, tax certificates, BTRs.
- **Permits:** City of Boca Raton, City of Delray Beach or PBC PZB portals; look for open/expired permits and unpermitted additions (sq ft on appraiser vs. permits).
- **Code enforcement** cases and liens by city.
- **Sunbiz** for LLC owners (managers, registered agent).
- **FEMA flood map** / city flood zone determination.

## Procedure
1. Confirm parcel ID (PCN) and jurisdiction.
2. Pull owner and sales history; note years owned, last price, absentee or out-of-state owner, LLC owner.
3. Search Clerk for all recordings on owner name and parcel; list open mortgages, liens, lis pendens and probate.
4. Pull permit and code history; flag open permits (they add to the 50% rule total) and violations.
5. Summarize red flags and items for the title company and attorney.

## Output
A records sheet: parcel facts, owner profile, encumbrances, permits/code, flood zone, red flags, next steps. Mark anything unverified. Do not publish owner personal data beyond business need.
