# Business tax & rental tax registrations

*Roster skill S26. Used by: `compliance-officer` (owner), `tax-strategist`, `property-manager`, `operations-director`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Register everything required and never miss a filing.

## Registrations
| Item | Who/where | When |
|---|---|---|
| County Local Business Tax Receipt | PBC Tax Collector | Operating business; renew annually by **Sept 30** |
| City BTR (Boca Raton / Delray Beach) | City business tax office | Operating location/activity; confirm each city's rules for residential rentals ⚠️ |
| Short-term rental BTR (county) | PBC Tax Collector, **per property** | Before any transient rental; renew by Sept 30 |
| Tourist Development Tax account | PBC Tax Collector | Rentals ≤ 6 months: **6%** of total rent incl. mandatory fees (cleaning, pet) |
| Florida sales tax certificate | Florida DOR | Transient rentals: 6% state + 0.5% PBC surtax = **6.5%** |
| DBPR vacation rental license | Florida DBPR | Units rented < 30 days more than 3 times/year |

- Booking platforms **do not remit PBC TDT**; we file and pay monthly (due on the 1st, late after the **20th**).
- Long-term leases > 6 months are exempt from sales tax and TDT.
- Delray Beach dropped its landlord permit requirement (Oct 1, 2023). Third-party claims of a Delray vacation-rental registration program are **unverified**; confirm with the city.

## Output
A registration checklist with status, owner and next due date, plus a monthly filing calendar.
