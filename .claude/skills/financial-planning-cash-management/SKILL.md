# Financial planning, budgeting & cash management

*Roster skill S19. Used by: `cfo-financial-planner` (owner), `chief-investment-strategist`, `capital-markets-advisor`, `operations-director`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Keep the company solvent, the books trustworthy and capital pointed at the best deals.

## Components
- **Operating budget:** salaries/contractors, software, marketing, legal/accounting, insurance, office (see `docs/05` and `docs/08`).
- **13-week cash forecast:** deal closings, draws, sales proceeds, rents, debt service, overhead.
- **Reserves policy:** company ≥ 6 months of overhead; per rehab 10–20% contingency; per rental 3–6 months of PITIA plus a capex reserve; extra hurricane-season liquidity.
- **Deal tracker:** budget vs. actual per project; realized vs. underwritten profit (feeds back into underwriting).
- **Portfolio report:** occupancy, rent roll, NOI, DSCR per loan, equity, LTV, maturity schedule.
- **Controls:** separate bank accounts per entity, two-person approval for wires (see `cybersecurity-wire-fraud-prevention`), monthly reconciliations, security deposits held per Ch. 83.

## Procedure
1. Gather actuals (bookkeeping), the pipeline (acquisitions) and the project budgets (renovation).
2. Update the forecast; flag a minimum-cash breach 8+ weeks out.
3. Recommend actions: slow acquisitions, sell or refi assets, raise capital, cut costs.

## Output
A budget or forecast table, KPI dashboard (cash runway, deals closed, avg profit, portfolio NOI/DSCR), variance commentary and recommendations.
