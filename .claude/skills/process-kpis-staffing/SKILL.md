# Process design, KPIs & staffing

*Roster skill S35. Used by: `operations-director` (owner), `chief-investment-strategist`, `cfo-financial-planner`, `proptech-architect`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Make the business run on repeatable processes so it can scale beyond the founders.

## Core workflow and stage gates
1. **Lead** (marketing) → 2. **Qualify & appointment** (acquisitions) → 3. **Underwrite** (all exits) → 4. **Offer** (within MAO) → 5. **Contract** → 6. **Due diligence gate** (inspections, insurance, flood, HOA, title) → 7. **Close** → 8. **Renovate** (scope, permits, draws) → 9. **Exit**: list/sell, lease, or BRRRR refi → 10. **Review** (actual vs. underwritten).
Each gate has an owner, a checklist and a go/no-go rule.

## KPI scorecard
Leads, cost per lead, appointments, offers, contracts, closings per month; average days lead→close; rehab schedule and cost variance; days on market; average flip profit and ROI; rental occupancy, delinquency, DSCR; cash runway.

## Staffing plan (from docs/08)
- Phase 1 (0–12 months, 4–10 deals): founder, acquisitions manager, project manager, licensed agent partner, transaction coordinator, VAs; outsourced bookkeeper, CPA, attorney, GCs, PM.
- Phase 2 (12–36 months, 15–30+ deals/year): dispositions manager, second acquisitions rep, marketing manager, in-house leasing or PM (broker license), maintenance tech, controller.
- Decide in-house vs. outsourced by volume, control and licensing needs.

## Output
A process map with owners and SLAs, SOP drafts, a scorecard definition, or a hiring plan with costs and timing.
