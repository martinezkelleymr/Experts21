# Lending & capital stack structuring

*Roster skill S18. Used by: `capital-markets-advisor` (owner), `cfo-financial-planner`, `chief-investment-strategist`, `investor-relations-manager`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Match each deal and the overall portfolio to the cheapest, safest capital that fits the timeline.

## Product cheat sheet (2026, verify quotes)
| Product | Terms | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Hard money / bridge | 10–13% IO, 1.5–2.5 pts, 12 months, up to 85–90% of purchase + 100% rehab, ≤ 70–75% of ARV | Flips, BRRRR buy |
| DSCR 30-yr | 6–8%, 20–25% down, DSCR ≥ 1.0–1.25, FICO 660–700+, 5-4-3-2-1 prepay | Rentals, BRRRR refi |
| Conventional investor | Market + 0.5–0.75%, 15–25% down, ≤ 10 financed properties | Principals' early rentals |
| Portfolio/bank line | Relationship pricing, blanket loans | Scaling |
| Private money | 8–12%, first lien, note + mortgage | Gap/equity |
| Transactional funding | 1–2% same-day | Double closings |

## Procedure
1. Define the need: amount, term, exit, speed, draw needs.
2. Get 2–3 term sheets; compare **all-in cost** (rate + points + fees + prepay) for the expected hold period.
3. Check: refi seasoning (3–6 months), appraisal risk, recourse/personal guarantee, extension fees, draw inspection fees.
4. Add Florida mortgage taxes: note doc stamps 0.35% + intangible tax 0.2% of the loan.
5. Stress-test the refi: if the appraisal comes in 10% low, how much cash is stuck?
6. Pooled investor money may be a security; route to `entity-structuring-securities`.

## Output
A lender comparison table, recommended capital stack (sources/uses), risks and a timeline.
