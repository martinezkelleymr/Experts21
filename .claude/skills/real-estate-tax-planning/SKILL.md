# Real estate tax planning

*Roster skill S20. Used by: `tax-strategist` (owner), `cfo-financial-planner`, `chief-investment-strategist`, `entity-securities-counsel`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Keep more of each dollar earned, legally, and budget property taxes correctly.

## Federal (confirm with the CPA; laws change)
- **Flips** are usually dealer property → ordinary income + self-employment tax; many investors use an **S-corp** flip entity to manage SE tax.
- **Rentals** held for investment → depreciation over 27.5 years (building only; land excluded), **cost segregation** and bonus depreciation where allowed, passive-activity rules (REP status exceptions), **1031 exchanges** (45/180-day rules; not available for dealer property).
- Keep flip and rental activity in **separate entities** to protect investor (capital gain/1031) treatment of rentals.
- Track basis: purchase, closing costs, capital improvements vs. repairs.

## Florida
- No state personal income tax; C-corps pay the Florida corporate income tax.
- **Property tax:** assessment resets to market value after a purchase (Jan 1 status). Non-homestead assessment-increase cap is **10%/yr through 2026 and 5%/yr from Jan 1, 2027** (Amendment 3, passed Nov 2026). Millage: Boca city ≈ 3.65 + county 4.5 + school and districts → total ≈ 17–19 mills; Delray city ≈ 6.16 → total ≈ 19–21 mills. Watch local millage hikes after Amendment 3.
- **TRIM** notice in August; petition the Value Adjustment Board by the deadline if overassessed.
- Doc stamps: deed 0.70% (customarily seller), note 0.35%, intangible 0.2% of the loan.
- Transient-rental taxes: see `business-rental-tax-registrations`.

## Output
A tax memo: the issue, options, estimated impact (with numbers), recommendation, CPA questions. **Always state that a licensed CPA must review before filing.**
