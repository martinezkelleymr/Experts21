# Florida building permits & code

*Roster skill S12. Used by: `permitting-code-specialist` (owner), `renovation-project-manager`, `compliance-officer`, `real-estate-attorney`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Get the work permitted correctly the first time and avoid liens, stop-work orders and failed resale inspections.

## Jurisdiction
- Incorporated Boca Raton → City of Boca Raton Development Services / Building.
- Incorporated Delray Beach → City of Delray Beach Building Division.
- Unincorporated "Boca/Delray" addresses → Palm Beach County Planning, Zoning & Building (PZB). Confirm on pbcpao.gov.

## Usually needs a permit
Roofing, windows/doors/shutters (product approvals, wind-load design), electrical (panels, circuits), plumbing (re-pipe, water heater, fixtures relocation), HVAC change-outs, structural or wall removal, additions/enclosures, pools/barriers, fences, driveways, generators, demolition. Cosmetic work (paint, flooring, cabinets in place) generally doesn't, but confirm locally.

## Key rules
- **Licensed contractors required** for flips and rentals: the owner-builder exemption (F.S. 489.103(7)) doesn't apply to buildings improved for sale or lease within 1 year. Verify licenses on myfloridalicense.com.
- **Notice of Commencement** recorded for improvements > $5,000 (> $15,000 for HVAC repair/replacement); collect partial and final **lien releases** before paying draws.
- Florida Building Code wind provisions; impact-rated openings or approved shutters in wind-borne debris regions.
- Flood zones: substantial-improvement (50%) rule; open permits add to the total.
- Historic districts need a Certificate of Appropriateness **before** a permit (see `historic-preservation-review`).
- Close out every permit (final inspection) before listing; buyers' lenders and title will check.

## Output
A permit plan: jurisdiction, permit list by trade, required documents (product approvals, drawings, NOC), expected review time, inspection sequence, risks.
