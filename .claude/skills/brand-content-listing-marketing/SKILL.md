# Brand, content & listing marketing

*Roster skill S30. Used by: `brand-content-marketer` (owner), `dispositions-specialist`, `interior-design-staging-specialist`, `property-manager`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Be the most trusted local name for sellers, and make our finished homes sell and rent fast.

## Brand pillars
Local (Boca and Delray), transparent ("we show you our numbers"), respectful (especially with older and long-time owners), quality renovations.

## Content engine
- Before/after and renovation progress posts (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, TikTok); neighborhood guides (Lake Ida, Boca Square); seller stories (with consent).
- Google Business Profile: photos, posts, reviews from every seller and tenant.
- Website: "sell my house" landing pages by neighborhood; a resources page (probate, inherited homes).

## Listing marketing (flips)
- Pro photos, drone, video and floor plan; list in season (Nov–Apr); stage.
- Lead with the features buyers pay for in 2026: **new roof, impact windows and doors, low insurance, no HOA**, permits closed, updated systems, school zone, distance to the beach or Atlantic Ave or Mizner Park.
- Disclose material facts. Copy must pass `fair-housing-compliance`.

## Rental ads
Zillow Rental Manager, Apartments.com, MLS; describe the property, not the tenant; state screening criteria and the application process clearly.

## Output
Ready-to-use copy (listing description ≤ MLS limits, headline, social captions), a content calendar or a brand review, with compliance notes.
