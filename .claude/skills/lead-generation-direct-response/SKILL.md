# Lead generation & direct-response marketing

*Roster skill S29. Used by: `lead-generation-marketer` (owner), `acquisitions-specialist`, `data-analytics-engineer`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Produce a steady, affordable flow of off-market deals inside the buy box.

## Lists (stack them: more overlapping signals = more motivation)
Absentee or out-of-state owners · owned 20+ years (older owners, likely equity) · probate/inherited · lis pendens/pre-foreclosure · tax-delinquent · code violations · vacant · expired/withdrawn listings · tired landlords (evictions, many code cases) · divorce. Build from PBCPAO, the Clerk and data tools (PropStream, BatchData, ATTOM) in target ZIPs (33431, 33432, 33486, 33487, 33444, 33445, 33483).

## Channel benchmarks
| Channel | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Direct mail | $0.60–$1.00/piece; ≈ 1–3 deals per 10K pieces | 4–6 touches, monthly cadence |
| PPC | $40–$150+/click; $300–$800/lead | Very competitive keywords |
| SEO / Google Business Profile | $1K–$5K/mo | Neighborhood pages, reviews; slow but cheap per lead |
| SMS/calls | VA + data | Compliance first (`marketing-compliance-tcpa-ftsa`) |
| Agents, wholesalers, attorneys | Relationship | Often 30–50% of deals |
| Auctions (PBC Clerk) | Cash | Title and occupancy risk |
| Bandit signs | Cheap | Often against city code; avoid |

## Procedure
1. Set targets: deals/month → contracts → offers → appointments → leads (use current conversion rates).
2. Back into budget per channel; A/B test mail pieces and ad copy.
3. Track by channel: cost per lead, cost per appointment, cost per contract, profit per deal, ROI.
4. Reallocate monthly to the best-performing channels.

## Output
A campaign plan (lists, channels, cadence, budget, KPIs, compliance notes) or a performance review with reallocations.
