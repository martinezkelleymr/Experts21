# Seller negotiation & offer strategy

*Roster skill S17. Used by: `acquisitions-specialist` (owner), `dispositions-specialist`, `chief-investment-strategist`. Market facts are a Nov 2026 snapshot from `docs/`; refresh before relying on them.*

## Purpose
Reach win-win agreements below retail by solving the seller's real problem: speed, certainty, convenience or terms.

## Discovery (ask before pricing)
Why sell, why now? Timeline? What happens if it doesn't sell? Who else decides (heirs, spouse, attorney)? Mortgage balance or liens? Condition and repairs known? What would make this easy for you?

## Offer structures
- Cash, fast close (7–21 days), as-is, we pay closing costs.
- Flexible close or leaseback for sellers who need time.
- Seller financing or terms when equity is high and the seller wants income.
- Subject-to (due-on-sale risk; attorney sign-off required).
- MLS: escalation is rare for investors; use speed and inspection waivers carefully.

## Rules
- Anchor with the MAO from `deal-underwriting-proforma`; never exceed it without CIS approval.
- Show the math transparently (ARV, repairs, costs) so the offer is credible.
- Post-inspection renegotiation: only for new, documented findings, with repair quotes.
- **Ethics and compliance:** no pressure tactics; take extra care with elderly or vulnerable sellers (suggest they involve family or an attorney); Florida contract rules; no misrepresenting licensed status (Ch. 475).

## Output
Seller profile, motivation hypothesis, offer options (price, terms, close date) with walk-away price, talk track, and likely objections with responses.
